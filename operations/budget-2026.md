## What this budget does *not* yet cover

- **Direct participant support** (seed contributions to founders per
  statutes Art. 2, mentorship stipends, etc. — see
  `participants/support-offered.md`) is **not sized here**. Cost per
  participant/track is still `[TBD]` in `participants/support-offered.md`;
  once that's set, it should become its own budget line (likely the
  largest one) rather than being absorbed into the ~20,000 operating
  figure above.
- **Paid staff.** The statutes assume an honorary/volunteer board (Art.
  17); no staff salaries are budgeted. Revisit if that changes.
- **Any statutory audit fee**, if the GA decides to appoint auditors
  (statutes Art. 14) — not yet costed.

## Reading this budget

At the low end (CHF 100,000 raised), the ~20,000 in operating costs
leaves roughly CHF 80,000 unallocated; at the high end (CHF 200,000),
roughly CHF 180,000. Until a participant-support cost-per-head figure
exists, that unallocated amount should be treated as **uncommitted** —
don't assume it's "spare" for other purposes without a board decision, since
it's the most likely source for funding the association's actual mission
(supporting founders and researchers) once that number is known.
