# Auf — Draft Budget, Years 1–3 (Q4 2026 – 2028)

**Status: draft for discussion — not yet approved by the board or GA.**
Figures are first-pass estimates from the founding team, to be refined
once fundraising and cost lines firm up. Amounts in Swiss Francs (CHF).

Every number below is derived from the two **assumption cells** in the
table right below — change those two rows and everything else follows.

## Assumptions

| Assumption | Q4 2026 | 2027 | 2028 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Spending basis — share of a full year's operating budget | 25% (Q4 only) | 100% (full year) | 100% (full year) |
| Fundraising basis — share of the CHF 100,000–200,000 target raised in this period | 100% | 0% | 0% |

- **Spending** is scaled by how much of the year is left: 2026 is
  already roughly half over, so this treats it as a **Q4-only stub** —
  one quarter of a full year's operating cost.
- **Fundraising** is *not* scaled the same way: this plan assumes Auf
  raises **all of its CHF 100,000–200,000 target in 2026** (interpreting
  "most of it in 2026" as effectively all, for simplicity), then spends
  down that reserve through 2027–2028 rather than raising fresh funds
  each year. If the real plan is closer to "80% in 2026, top up
  afterward," change the fundraising row and the numbers below follow.

## Summary

| | Q4 2026 | 2027 | 2028 | 3-year total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Fundraising raised — low** (100,000 × row above) | 100,000 | 0 | 0 | 100,000 |
| **Fundraising raised — high** (200,000 × row above) | 200,000 | 0 | 0 | 200,000 |
| **Operating expenses** (20,000 × row above) | 5,000 | 20,000 | 20,000 | 45,000 |
| **Reserve movement — low** | +95,000 | −20,000 | −20,000 | +55,000 |
| **Reserve movement — high** | +195,000 | −20,000 | −20,000 | +155,000 |
| **Cumulative reserve, end of period — low** | 95,000 | 75,000 | 55,000 | — |
| **Cumulative reserve, end of period — high** | 195,000 | 175,000 | 155,000 | — |

Because fundraising is front-loaded into 2026 while spending continues
through 2027–2028, 2027 and 2028 show a *negative* reserve movement —
that's expected under this assumption (living off the 2026 raise), not a
funding shortfall, as long as the cumulative reserve stays positive. It
does here in both cases (down to CHF 55,000 low-case by end of 2028).

## What this budget does *not* yet cover

- **Direct participant support** (seed contributions to founders per
  statutes Art. 2, mentorship stipends, etc. — see
  `participants/support-offered.md`) is **not sized here, in any
  period**. Cost per participant/track is still `[TBD]`; once set, it
  becomes its own line — likely the largest cost driver by 2027 or 2028
  if Auf runs more than one cohort, and it would eat directly into the
  reserve shown above rather than requiring fresh fundraising, under the
  current assumption.
- **Paid staff.** Statutes assume an honorary/volunteer board (Art. 17);
  no salaries budgeted in any period. If Auf hires anyone across this
  horizon, this whole plan needs revisiting.
- **Any statutory audit fee**, if the GA appoints auditors (statutes
  Art. 14) — not yet costed for any period.
- **Whether "raise it all in 2026, spend it down after" is actually the
  right fundraising strategy.** It's a clean assumption for modeling
  purposes, but real donor relationships (see `donors/strategy.md`) may
  favor an ongoing annual ask over one big upfront raise — worth a
  deliberate decision, not just accepting this default.

## Reading this plan

The reserve, not the per-period fundraising number, is the thing to
watch under this assumption: 2027 and 2028 are *designed* to show CHF 0
raised and a negative movement, because the plan assumes 2026 already
raised enough to cover them. Don't read those zeroes as a fundraising
failure — but do treat the **cumulative reserve row** as the real budget
constraint: if a new cost (participant funding, rent, staff) shows up
before 2028, it comes out of that reserve, and the reserve could go
negative well before the "3-year total" looks concerning.

- [ ] Set a cost-per-participant figure in
  `participants/support-offered.md` and add it as a budget line, ideally
  per period (cohorts may scale up) — this is what would actually draw
  down the reserve shown above.
- [ ] Approve this plan via board resolution and/or GA per
  `operations/finance.md`'s budget cycle — note that the statutes'
  annual-budget process (Art. 12) is inherently a one-year cycle, so a
  multi-year/partial-year plan like this would need to be re-approved or
  re-confirmed annually rather than adopted once and left alone.
