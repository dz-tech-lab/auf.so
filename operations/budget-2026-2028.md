# Auf — Draft Budget, Years 1–3 (2026–2028)

**Status: draft for discussion — not yet approved by the board or GA.**
Figures are first-pass estimates from the founding team, to be refined
once fundraising and cost lines firm up. Amounts in Swiss Francs (CHF).

**Assumption flagged up front**: only Year 1's numbers came from the
founding team (fundraising target CHF 100,000–200,000; ~20,000/year on
events + office equipment). Years 2 and 3 simply repeat the Year 1
figures as a flat placeholder — there's no actual multi-year fundraising
or cost plan yet. Treat everything past Year 1 as illustrative until the
founding team sets real assumptions (growth in donor base, inflation on
costs, planned headcount, etc.).

## What this budget does *not* yet cover

- **Direct participant support** (seed contributions to founders per
  statutes Art. 2, mentorship stipends, etc. — see
  `participants/support-offered.md`) is **not sized here, in any year**.
  Cost per participant/track is still `[TBD]`; once set, it becomes its
  own line — likely the largest cost driver by Year 2 or 3 if Auf runs
  more than one cohort.
- **Paid staff.** Statutes assume an honorary/volunteer board (Art. 17);
  no salaries budgeted in any year. If Auf hires anyone across this
  3-year horizon, this whole plan needs revisiting.
- **Any statutory audit fee**, if the GA appoints auditors (statutes
  Art. 14) — not yet costed for any year.
- **Growth or decline in fundraising capacity.** Year 1 is a genuine
  estimate; Years 2–3 are the same number copy-pasted forward, not a
  forecast. A real donor base typically doesn't stay flat — first-year
  fundraising is usually harder (no track record yet) and later years
  can go either way depending on whether Auf delivers visible outcomes.

## Reading this plan

Don't treat the "3-year total net" row as CHF 240,000–540,000 sitting
available for discretionary spending. It's the residual after only the
costs that are currently sized (events, equipment, no rent, no
participant funding, no staff). The real test of this plan is whether
the founding team sets a per-participant cost and a realistic multi-year
fundraising trajectory — until then, this is a placeholder shell, not a
financial plan the board should rely on for a 3-year commitment.
