## ⚠️ Key finding: the low end of the fundraising target may not be enough

Once beneficiary grants are sized in (per counsel's suggested scaling —
see below), the **low-case fundraising target of CHF 100,000 does not
cover three years of operations**: the cumulative reserve goes
**negative by end of 2028** (≈ **−CHF 56,000**). The high-case target
(CHF 200,000) survives, but only with a thin ≈CHF 24,000 buffer left by
end of 2028. This wasn't visible before grants were included as a real
expense line — see "Reading this plan" below for what to do about it.

## Assumptions

| Assumption | Q4 2026 | 2027 | 2028 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Spending basis — share of a full year's *operating* budget (events, equipment, coworking, SaaS) | 25% (Q4 only) | 100% (full year) | 100% (full year) |
| Fundraising basis — share of the CHF 100,000–200,000 target raised in this period | 100% | 0% | 0% |

- **Operating spend** (events/equipment/coworking/SaaS) is scaled by how
  much of the year is left: 2026 is already roughly half over, so this
  treats it as a **Q4-only stub** — a quarter of a full year's cost.
- **Grants to beneficiaries** are *not* scaled this way — grants are
  lumpy (you fund a discrete company, not a fraction of one), so they're
  given directly per period below, following counsel's suggested
  ramp-up.
- **Fundraising** is also *not* scaled the same way as operating spend:
  this plan assumes Auf raises **all of its CHF 100,000–200,000 target
  in 2026**, then draws down that reserve through 2027–2028 rather than
  raising fresh funds each year. Change this assumption if the real plan
  is to keep fundraising every year — see the key finding above for why
  that may be necessary anyway.

## Beneficiary grants — counsel's suggested ramp-up

Per counsel, grounded in the Statement of Purpose (max. 10 supported
companies, grants up to CHF 10,000 each): a first full year at 4–5
funded units (CHF 40,000–50,000), a second full year at 7–8 units (CHF
70,000–80,000), continuing to ramp toward the 10-company cap after that.

Mapped onto this budget's periods (my interpretation — flagged since
counsel's "Jahr 1/2" assumed a full first year, while this plan treats
2026 as a partial pre-ramp stub):

| Period | Units funded | Grant expense |
|---|---:|---:|
| Q4 2026 (pre-ramp stub) | ~1 (my own placeholder — counsel didn't size a partial first quarter) | ~10,000 |
| 2027 (= counsel's "Jahr 1") | 4–5 | 40,000–50,000 |
| 2028 (= counsel's "Jahr 2") | 7–8 | 70,000–80,000 |

**Confirm the Q4 2026 figure with the founding team** — funding even one
company within Auf's first active quarter as a brand-new association
may be optimistic or exactly right, depending on how far along
conversations already are.

## What this budget does *not* yet cover

- **Paid staff.** Statutes assume an honorary/volunteer board (Art. 17);
  no salaries budgeted in any period. If Auf hires anyone across this
  horizon, this whole plan needs revisiting.
- **Any statutory audit fee**, if the GA appoints auditors (statutes
  Art. 14) — not yet costed for any period.
- **Whether "raise it all in 2026, spend it down after" is actually the
  right fundraising strategy.** See the key finding above — under this
  assumption, the low-case target isn't enough once grants are real.

## Reading this plan

This is the first version of this budget that treats beneficiary grants
as a real, sized expense rather than an unsized future line — and doing
so changes the conclusion. Three ways to close the gap, none applied
here yet because they're the founding team's call:

1. **Fundraise above CHF 100,000**, or specifically target the higher
   end of the range, if the grant ramp-up above is the real plan.
2. **Slow the grant ramp-up** — e.g. fewer than 7–8 funded units in
   2028, or smaller grants than the CHF 10,000 cap per unit.
3. **Keep fundraising in 2027 and 2028** instead of assuming one 2026
   raise covers everything — change the "Fundraising basis" assumption
   row so 2027/2028 aren't 0%.

Whichever combination is chosen, re-run this table — the **cumulative
reserve** row is the number to keep positive, not the 3-year total.
