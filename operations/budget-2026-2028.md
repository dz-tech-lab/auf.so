# Auf — Draft Budget, Years 1–3 (Q4 2026 – 2028)

**Internal status**: reviewed by external counsel (Schellenberg Wittmer,
2026-07-16) for tax-exemption purposes; **still pending formal board/GA
budget approval** per `operations/finance.md`'s budget cycle. Amounts in
Swiss Francs (CHF). *If this document is used in an actual submission
packet, remove this status line first.*

Every number below is derived from a small set of **assumption cells**
— change those and everything else follows.

## Updated finding: the low case is now viable, but only just

With the revised fundraising schedule (raised across 2026 **and** 2027,
not all at once) and a trimmed 2028 grant count in the low case, the
**cumulative reserve stays positive throughout — but only by CHF 4,000**
at the end of 2028 in the low case. The high case ends with a much
healthier ≈CHF 124,000 buffer. This is a large improvement over the
previous version of this plan (which went negative by ≈CHF 56,000 in
the low case) — see "Reading this plan" below for why, and how thin the
low-case margin still is.

## Assumptions

| Assumption | Q4 2026 | 2027 | 2028 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Spending basis — share of a full year's *operating* budget (events, equipment, coworking, SaaS) | 25% (Q4 only) | 100% (full year) | 100% (full year) |
| Fundraising basis — share of the CHF 100,000–200,000 target raised this period | 50% | 100% | 0% |

- **Operating spend** is unchanged from the previous version — scaled by
  how much of the year is left (Q4 2026 = a quarter of a full year's
  cost).
- **Fundraising** is now spread across two periods instead of a single
  2026 raise: **50% of the target in Q4 2026, the remaining 100% in
  2027** (2027's row is 100% of the *base* target, not "100% of what's
  left" — see the numbers below), **nothing further assumed in 2028.**
  Low case: CHF 50,000 in Q4 2026 + CHF 100,000 in 2027 = CHF 150,000
  total. High case: CHF 100,000 in Q4 2026 + CHF 200,000 in 2027 = CHF
  300,000 total. Both are larger 3-year totals than the previous
  "raise it all in 2026" version (CHF 100,000 / 200,000), because this
  schedule assumes fundraising continues into 2027 rather than being a
  single one-time raise.

## What this budget does *not* yet cover

- **Paid staff.** Statutes assume an honorary/volunteer board (Art. 17);
  no salaries budgeted in any period.
- **Any statutory audit fee**, if the GA appoints auditors (statutes
  Art. 14) — not yet costed.
- **Fundraising in 2028.** This plan assumes no new money is raised in
  2028 — if that turns out to be wrong (in either direction), the
  cumulative reserve changes accordingly, and the low case in
  particular has almost no room to absorb a shortfall.

## Reading this plan

The low case now survives the full three years, but just barely: CHF
4,000 left at the end of 2028 is not a buffer, it's a rounding error.
Anything that pushes cost up or fundraising down even slightly — one
more beneficiary grant, a slower 2027 raise, a lease instead of
coworking — puts the low case into negative territory. Treat the low
case as "break-even, no margin for error," not as a comfortably solved
scenario. The high case has real room (≈CHF 124,000 at the end of
2028), which is the more realistic cushion to plan around if fundraising
lands anywhere above the low end.
