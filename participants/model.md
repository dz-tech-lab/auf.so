# Participant model

Auf supports people building things connected to the ETH/Zurich ecosystem —
this is deliberately **not limited to "founders" or "researchers" as the
only two categories.** The model below is a flexible per-cohort framework,
not a fixed taxonomy to hard-code anywhere else in this repo (docs,
templates, issue forms).

## Tracks are per-cohort, not fixed

Each cohort defines its own set of tracks based on who applies and what
makes sense for that cohort. Tracks seen so far or anticipated include:

- **Founder** — building a company or venture.
- **Researcher** — pursuing research that may or may not commercialize.
- **Mixed / hybrid** — e.g. a researcher spinning out a venture, or a
  founder whose work is research-heavy.
- **[Other]** — the model should accommodate whatever doesn't fit the
  above; don't force a participant into "founder" or "researcher" if
  neither fits. Add new track labels as needed per cohort.

When defining a cohort (see `planning/`), state its actual track mix
explicitly rather than assuming the two obvious categories.

## What varies by track / cohort

- **Support package** — see `participants/support-offered.md`; not every
  track needs the same mix of funding, mentorship, space, network.
- **Application questions** — see `participants/application.md`; the
  issue form should ask track-appropriate questions, not one-size-fits-all
  ones.
- **Milestones / success criteria** — a founder's milestones (fundraising,
  users) differ from a researcher's (publication, grant, spin-off
  decision). Define these per cohort rather than assuming one definition
  of "success."
