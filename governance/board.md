## Roles

- **President**: chairs board meetings and the GA, primary external
  representative, casting vote on ties (per statutes, if applicable).
- **Treasurer**: owns `operations/finance.md`, prepares annual accounts
  for GA approval, oversees donor receipts (`donors/receipts.md`).
- **Secretary**: takes and files minutes (`governance/minutes/`), maintains
  this repo's governance docs, manages board resolution paperwork.

## Resolution process

1. Any board member may propose a resolution (in a meeting or, for urgent
   matters, by circular/written resolution).
2. Discussion happens in a board meeting or, for written resolutions, over
   [email/Slack — TBD channel] with a clear deadline to object.
3. Resolutions pass by [simple majority of the board — TBD; confirm
   against statutes Art. 5].
4. Every resolution is recorded using
   `governance/templates/board-resolution-template.md` and filed under
   `governance/minutes/`.
5. Resolutions affecting signing authority, bank mandates, or the entity
   registry must also update `governance/entity-registry.md` in the same
   PR.
