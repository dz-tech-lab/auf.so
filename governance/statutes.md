# Statuten / Statutes — [Auf, Verein] (draft skeleton)

**Status: DRAFT skeleton, not filed, not adopted.** This follows the
structure required for a Swiss association (Verein) under Art. 60ff. of the
Swiss Civil Code (ZGB). It is a starting skeleton for the founding team and
counsel to fill in and adopt at the constituent general assembly (Gründungs-
versammlung) — it is not a substitute for legal review.

Sections mirror the minimum content a Verein's statutes must cover.

## Art. 8 — Dissolution and asset lock (Auflösung)

The Association may be dissolved by GA resolution with a majority of
[2/3 of members present — TBD], per Art. 76 ZGB.

In the event of dissolution, and consistent with maintaining
tax-exempt/gemeinnützig status (see `governance/tax-status.md`), any
remaining assets **after settlement of liabilities shall not be
distributed to members** but shall be transferred to another
tax-exempt, non-profit organization pursuing a similar purpose, as
determined by the final GA. Under no circumstances shall assets revert to
founders, members, or board members.
