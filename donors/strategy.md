# Donor strategy

Who we ask, how we ask, and how often — the policy layer. Live pipeline
status for individual donors lives in GitHub Issues, not here (see
`donors/pipeline.md`).

## Who we ask

The founding team maintains a stakeholder relationship CRM organized into
four categories — the same categories should be used as the `donor/type-*`
labels described in `donors/pipeline.md`:

- **Founders**: successful/exited founders in the Zurich ecosystem — as
  donors, mentors, or connectors. Note: this is a *donor/supporter*
  category, distinct from the "Founder" **track** in
  `participants/model.md`, which describes incoming program
  participants — don't conflate the two.
- **Family businesses**: multi-generational Swiss businesses and family
  offices with a philanthropic history, often already active in ETH or
  Zurich-area giving.
- **Investors**: VCs and angels who back ETH-adjacent spinouts — natural
  co-travelers even when a gift isn't the primary ask.
- **Political / government contacts**: cantonal or federal contacts
  focused on innovation and economic policy. These relationships are
  often about advocacy and ecosystem access rather than a direct
  financial ask — track them here anyway so the relationship isn't lost,
  but don't assume every political contact is a donor prospect.

This list mirrors an existing spreadsheet CRM the founding team built;
the intent is to move that same categorization into the Issues-based
pipeline (`donors/pipeline.md`) rather than maintain both. The
spreadsheet itself should not be committed to this repo — it contains
real names, emails, and notes on specific people, which is exactly the
kind of personal data `governance/data-protection.md` says must never go
into git.

## Cadence

- **Prospecting**: ongoing, opportunistic + targeted outreach ahead of
  each cohort.
- **Ask**: timed around [cohort kickoff / annual fundraising cycle — TBD].
- **Stewardship**: quarterly updates + annual report, per
  `donors/reporting.md`.

## Principles

- Every ask is tracked as a GitHub Issue from first contact (see
  `donors/pipeline.md`) — no donor relationship should exist only in
  someone's inbox.
- No donor's payment or financial details are ever recorded in this repo
  — see `governance/data-protection.md`.
- Tax-deductibility claims in outreach must match confirmed status in
  `governance/tax-status.md` — don't promise deductibility before it's
  confirmed.
