# Annual report outline

Section-by-section outline for the annual donor/public report. Fill in
per fiscal year; keep past years as separate dated files if useful
(e.g. `donors/comms/annual-report-2026.md`) rather than overwriting this
outline.

1. **Letter from the board** — short, personal, sets the tone.
2. **What Auf is** — pull from `strategy/narrative.md`, kept current.
3. **The year in numbers** — cohorts run, participants supported
   (aggregate, de-identified), funds raised (aggregate), key milestones.
4. **Participant outcomes** — de-identified stories/aggregate outcomes
   pulled with consent from `participants/alumni.md`; never include
   details an alumnus hasn't agreed to share publicly.
5. **Financial summary** — high-level, consistent with the GA-approved
   annual accounts; no per-donor amounts.
6. **Thank you to donors** — by tier or individually, per each donor's
   recognition preference (see `donors/records/`).
7. **Looking ahead** — next cohort plans, from `planning/`.

## Rules

- Every fact about a specific participant or donor needs their consent to
  publish — check `participants/profiles/` / `donors/records/` notes
  before including anything identifiable.
- No PII, ever — same rule as everywhere else in this repo, see
  `governance/data-protection.md`.
