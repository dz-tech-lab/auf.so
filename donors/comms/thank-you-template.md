# Thank-you template

Send promptly after a donor reaches `donor/received` in the pipeline (see
`donors/pipeline.md`), alongside the formal receipt (see
`donors/receipts.md`).

---

Subject: Thank you — [donor/org name] x Auf

Dear [name],

Thank you for your generous support of Auf. [One specific sentence about
what this contribution enables — a participant's support package, a
cohort activity, general operations — pulled from
`participants/support-offered.md` or `donors/strategy.md` tiers.]

[If applicable: recognition preferences — "As agreed, we'll [list you
publicly as.../keep this contribution anonymous]."]

You'll hear from us with a quarterly update on how things are going, and
a full annual report after our fiscal year closes — see
`donors/reporting.md` for our cadence.

[If receipt attached separately: "Please find your donation receipt
attached / to follow separately."]

With thanks,
[name], [role] — Auf

---

*This is a comms template, not a legal receipt — see
`donors/receipts.md` for the formal acknowledgment process.*
