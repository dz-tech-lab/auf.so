# Outreach template

Starting point for first-contact donor outreach. Adjust tone per
relationship — this is a skeleton, not a script to send verbatim.

---

Subject: [Auf x <donor/org name>]

Hi [name],

[One line of genuine, specific connection — how you know them or why
you're reaching out to them specifically.]

I'm reaching out on behalf of Auf, a Swiss association supporting
founders and researchers connected to the ETH/Zurich ecosystem. [One or
two sentences from `strategy/narrative.md` — the canonical description.]

[One concrete example of what support looks like — pull from
`participants/support-offered.md`, de-identified.]

[The ask — a specific tier if relevant, or an open invitation to a call.
See `donors/strategy.md` for tiers.]

[If tax status confirmed: note deductibility per
`governance/tax-status.md`. If not yet confirmed, do not promise
deductibility.]

Would you be open to a short call?

Best,
[name], [role] — Auf

---

*After sending, create/update the donor's tracking Issue per
`donors/pipeline.md` (label `donor/contacted`).*
