# Receipts

Policy for acknowledging donations, and a point-in-time log of receipts
issued (not amounts or payment details — see below).

## Process

1. On a donor reaching `donor/received` (see `donors/pipeline.md`), the
   treasurer issues a receipt/acknowledgment letter within [X business
   days — TBD].
2. If gemeinnützig status is confirmed (see `governance/tax-status.md`),
   the receipt should state the Association's tax-exempt status and any
   language required for the donor's own tax deduction.
3. Log the receipt below with a reference, not amounts.
